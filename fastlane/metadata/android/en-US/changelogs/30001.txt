Calls: the screen turns off at your ear (switch it off in Settings if your sensor misbehaves), USSD codes like *123# work, and blocking a number asks first. The app opens on Calls, with All/Missed/Incoming/Outgoing filters; long-press a favourite to reorder. Dialpad: paste a copied number, or call, WhatsApp or Signal a number that isn't saved; chat links add your country code. Calls under 3 seconds no longer count toward a bond.
